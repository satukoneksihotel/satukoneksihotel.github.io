Quick alerts for every trip

1) Fill in the official (free) arrival card in the last 3 days before you fly. 2) Check your visa on the official immigration site, not a copycat. 3) Never carry drugs - penalties in Southeast Asia are among the harshest in the world. 4) Buy travel insurance. 5) Save the emergency numbers and your embassy's contact. 6) Dress modestly at religious sites and ask before photographing people.
