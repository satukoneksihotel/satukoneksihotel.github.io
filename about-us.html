About Us

Your Multi-Hotel Room Reservation Specialist

Welcome to PT Satu Koneksi Hotel, where we take the stress out of finding the perfect stay.

We are your dedicated multi-hotel room reservation specialist—curating the best room options across our extensive hotel network and bringing them directly to you.

Forget the endless hours of scrolling through countless booking sites. Just tell us where and when you want to stay—then we will compare hotels for you and send you the best options for your budget, preferences, and needs. Whether you are traveling for business, on a family vacation, or on a weekend getaway, we do the heavy lifting so you can focus on enjoying the journey.

Book with Absolute Confidence

In the travel industry, trust and security are everything. We want you to book with peace of mind, knowing you are dealing with a verified, accountable, and professional organization.

PT Satu Koneksi Hotel is an officially registered company, legally recognized under the laws of the Republic of Indonesia. We operate transparently and are officially listed in the government's Online Single Submission (OSS) system for Risk-Based Business Licensing.

How We Work for You

  1. You tell us what you need. Share your destination, travel dates, and any specific preferences (for example, a pool view, near public transport, or family-friendly).
  2. We curate. Our reservation experts use our wide network to compare hotels, rates, and room amenities in real time.
  3. You choose. We send you a shortlist of the best options, hand-picked for you. Choose your favorite, and we will secure the booking smoothly.
