Belitung: 9 places for granite beaches, island hopping and Laskar Pelangi

The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

🏞️#01Granite beachNorth coast, Sijuk

Tanjung Tinggi Beach

Smooth granite boulders stand in turquoise shallow water along a white-sand beach. It was a filming location for the movie Laskar Pelangi, so locals also call it the Laskar Pelangi beach.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-45 min from Tanjung Pandan
  • 🌅 Morning or late afternoon
Our tipClimb the rocks carefully, as they can be slippery when wet. Go in the morning or late afternoon for softer light and fewer people, and swim only in calm, shallow water.
#02Boat gatewayNorth coast, Sijuk

Tanjung Kelayang & sunset

The main departure point for island-hopping boats, with a long beach, granite rocks and a calm bay. Many visitors return in the late afternoon to watch the sun go down over the water.

  • ⏱ 1-2 hours (or the whole boat day)
  • 🚗 about 30-60 min from Tanjung Pandan
  • 🌅 Late afternoon for sunset
Our tipBook boats through a registered local operator and ask for life jackets before you leave the jetty. Check sea conditions that morning, as wind can make crossings rough.
📷 JS Barry · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03LighthouseOff Tanjung Kelayang

Lengkuas Island Lighthouse

A white steel lighthouse from the Dutch colonial era, built in the 1880s, stands over a small island of pale sand and granite. Climb it for a wide view of the reefs and nearby islets.

  • ⏱ 1-2 hours on the island
  • 🚗 about 30 min by boat from Tanjung Kelayang
  • 🌅 Morning, calm seas
Our tipThe climb is by many narrow steps, so take your time and keep children close. Snorkelling here is best at calm times; stay near the beach and follow your boat crew's advice.
📷 26Isabella · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#04Granite isletsOff Tanjung Kelayang

Batu Berlayar & Burung Island

Small islets of giant granite rocks on pale sand. At Batu Berlayar you can walk between the boulders, and Burung Island is named after a rock shaped like a bird's head.

  • ⏱ A stop in the island-hopping day
  • 🚗 about 15-30 min by boat from Tanjung Kelayang
  • 🌅 Morning to midday
Our tipWear sandals with grip, as the rocks and sand can be hot and slippery. Stops, order and timing depend on tide and weather, so let the boat crew decide the safest route.
🏞️#05SandbankOff Tanjung Kelayang

Pasir Island sandbank & snorkelling

A sandbar in the sea that shows only at certain tide levels, with shallow clear water where starfish are often seen. Reefs around the nearby islets offer easy snorkelling.

  • ⏱ A stop in the island-hopping day
  • 🚗 about 20-30 min by boat from Tanjung Kelayang
  • 🌅 Low tide, calm seas
Our tipThe sandbar depends on the tide, so ask your boat crew for the best time. Look but do not take starfish out of the water, and never stand on coral.
#06Mining lakeTanjung Pandan

Kaolin Lake (Danau Kaolin)

A former kaolin (white clay) mine that rainwater filled, leaving a pale blue-green lake against white banks. It is a favourite for photos and shows how mining shaped the island.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 in and around Tanjung Pandan
  • 🌅 Morning or late afternoon
Our tipDo not swim: local authorities discourage it and the ground at the edge can be soft and unstable. Keep to marked areas and stay back from the water's edge.
📷 Ayufib · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#07MuseumTanjung Pandan

Tanjung Pandan Museum

Belitung's regency museum began with the island's tin-mining company in 1962. Its rooms cover mineral samples, old mining methods, ceramics from shipwrecks and daily life on the island.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 in the town centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipCheck opening days before going. Explanations are mostly in Indonesian, so a guide helps. The small animal enclosure behind it gets poor reviews, so many visitors skip it.
🏞️#08Laskar PelangiGantong, East Belitung

Andrea Hirata Museum of Words (Gantong)

Indonesia's first literary museum, in the home village of author Andrea Hirata, celebrates the novel Laskar Pelangi. Its bright rainbow-coloured rooms are inspired by the little school in the story.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours with nearby sights
  • 🚗 about 1-2 hrs east of Tanjung Pandan
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipThe school building people photograph nearby is a replica made for the film, not the original. Start early and combine it with a coffee stop in Manggar town.
🏞️#09FoodTanjung Pandan & Manggar

Belitung food & coffee shops

Try gangan, a yellow sour-spicy fish soup, mie Belitung with a shrimp-based gravy, grilled fresh seafood and a slow cup of coffee in a traditional warung kopi, especially in Manggar.

  • ⏱ A meal or two
  • 🚗 in Tanjung Pandan and Manggar
  • 🌅 Lunch and dinner
Our tipMost food is halal, but some Chinese-Indonesian eateries may not be - ask first if it matters. Ask us for places we know and check seafood freshness and price before ordering.

Practical tips

Getting there

Fly to H.A.S. Hanandjoeddin Airport in Tanjung Pandan; flights from Jakarta take about an hour. Tanjung Kelayang and Tanjung Tinggi are on the north coast, roughly 30-60 minutes by road from town and airport.

Best time

The dry season, roughly April to October, is best for boat trips and snorkelling, though wind can make the sea choppy. School holidays (June-July) and weekends are busier. Keep a spare day in case a boat trip is moved for weather.

Respect & dress

Belitung is mostly Muslim, with a Chinese-Indonesian community too. Beachwear is fine at the beach, but cover up in towns, mosques and temples. Ask before photographing people, and ask about halal if it matters to you.

Getting around

Public transport is limited and sights are spread out. A car with driver is the easiest way to combine them; boats for the islands leave from Tanjung Kelayang. Always wear the life jacket on the water.
