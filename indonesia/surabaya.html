The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01LandmarkGenteng

Heroes Monument & Museum

The tall, ribbed monument honours the battle of 10 November 1945 that gave Surabaya its nickname. An underground museum next to it tells the story with dioramas and photographs.

  • ⏱ 1.5-2 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipGo in the morning before the heat builds, and check the official site for current visiting hours. 10 November is Heroes' Day, when the area is busy with ceremonies.
📷 Ivuvisual · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#02MuseumKrembangan

House of Sampoerna

A Dutch-era complex that once housed an orphanage and later became a famous cigarette maker's first factory. Today it is a museum, with a viewing gallery above the hand-rolling workers.

  • ⏱ 1.5 hours
  • 🚗 about 15-20 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipTours are usually guided and the on-site cafe is a pleasant break. Hours and tour times can change, so check the official page for your date.
📷 consigliere ivan from Bontang, Indonesia · CC BY-SA 2.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03Old townAmpel, Semampir

Ampel Mosque & the Old Town

One of the oldest mosques in Java, tied to the early spread of Islam, stands in the lanes of the Arab quarter, with stalls of dates, spices and perfume. Old Dutch trading buildings are nearby.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 20-30 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipDress modestly (women cover hair and arms where asked), avoid prayer times for sightseeing inside, and ask before taking photos of worshippers. The lanes are crowded, so keep bags close.
📷 consigliere ivan from Bontang, Indonesia · CC BY 2.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#04LandmarkKenjeran

Suramadu Bridge

Indonesia's longest bridge, about 5.4 km, links Surabaya with Madura island over the Madura Strait. Many visitors come for sunset views and a first taste of Madura.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 about 30-40 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Late afternoon / sunset
Our tipStopping on the bridge is not allowed for safety - enjoy the view from the Surabaya-side park or viewpoint instead. Traffic gets heavy around rush hour and holidays.
📷 Sakurai Midori · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#05SeasideKenjeran

Kenjeran Coast & Sanggar Agung Temple

Surabaya's seaside strip: Kenjeran Park, a colourful bridge over the water and the Sanggar Agung temple, with a large sea-facing statue. Locals come for snacks, sea air and family outings.

  • ⏱ 2 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-40 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Late afternoon
Our tipThe water is brown and not meant for swimming - come for walks and food. Weekends and holidays are crowded, so weekday afternoons are calmer. Respect worshippers inside the temple.
📷 Okkisafire · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#06MuseumGenteng

Submarine Monument (Monkasel)

A real retired navy submarine, KRI Pasopati, now open to walk through on the bank of the Kalimas River - a fun stop for families and anyone curious about life on board.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipPassages and ladders inside are narrow, so wear comfortable clothes and shoes; taller visitors should mind their heads. Check the official source for visiting hours.
#07Old streetsGenteng

Tunjungan Street & Majapahit Hotel

Surabaya's grand old shopping street, lined with restored colonial-era buildings, murals, cafes and the historic Majapahit Hotel - the site of the 1945 flag incident.

  • ⏱ 2 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Evening
Our tipEvenings are cooler and the street lights up for photos. Cross carefully, as traffic is busy, and keep valuables out of sight when walking.
📷 consigliere ivan · CC BY 2.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#08MosqueGenteng

Cheng Ho Mosque

A mosque with a Chinese-style pagoda roof in red, green and gold, named after the Muslim admiral Zheng He - a lovely sign of Indonesia's mix of cultures.

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • 🚗 about 10-15 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipVisitors are generally welcome outside prayer times; cover shoulders and knees and remove shoes. Ask a caretaker if you may enter or photograph inside.
#09Day tripMojokerto

Trawas & Jolotundo Temple (Penanggungan)

Cool mountain air in the Trawas hills, with Jolotundo, an ancient bathing place on the slope of Mount Penanggungan, and many small ruins of old Javanese temples in the area.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 about 1.5-2 hrs from the centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipTrails on Penanggungan are steep and slippery after rain - hire a local guide for any hike. Go on a weekday for a quieter visit and dress modestly at the sacred site.
📷 Cun Cun · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Practical tips

Getting there

Juanda International Airport (SUB) is south of the city - allow about 30-60 minutes by road depending on traffic. Trains from Jakarta, Yogyakarta and Malang arrive at Gubeng, Pasar Turi or Surabaya Kota stations.

Best time

The dry season (roughly April to October) is easiest for sightseeing. Surabaya is hot all year, so plan outdoor visits for early morning or late afternoon and carry water.

Respect & dress

Surabaya is mostly Muslim and halal food is easy to find; mosques and prayer rooms are widespread. Cover shoulders and knees at mosques and temples, remove shoes, and ask before photographing people.

Getting around

Ride-hailing apps and metered taxis are the simplest way around town; traffic is heavy in rush hours. For Trawas or a full-day trip, a car with driver is easiest. Agree the price first for any unmetered ride.
