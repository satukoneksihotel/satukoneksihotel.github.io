The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01UNESCOMagelang

Borobudur Temple

The world's largest Buddhist temple, built in the 9th century: nine stone terraces, 2,672 relief panels and 72 bell-shaped stupas with Merapi on the horizon.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from the city
  • 🌅 Sunrise / early morning
Our tipClimbing to the upper terraces needs a special ticket with a daily limit and a guide - book ahead on the official site. For sunrise, many visitors go to Punthuk Setumbu hill nearby.
📷 CEphoto, Uwe Aranas · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#02UNESCOSleman

Prambanan Temple

Indonesia's largest Hindu temple compound from the 9th century, with soaring towers for Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma and finely carved Ramayana reliefs.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-45 min from the city
  • 🌅 Late afternoon
Our tipStay for the Ramayana Ballet - performed on the open-air stage in the dry season and indoors at other times. Check the schedule before you go.
📷 Christopher Michel · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03Royal heritageCity centre

Kraton & Taman Sari Water Castle

The Sultan's palace is still the home of Yogyakarta's royal family. A short walk away, Taman Sari is the 18th-century royal garden with bathing pools and an underground mosque.

  • ⏱ 3 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipBoth open mainly in the morning and close early - go before noon. Dress modestly and keep shoulders covered inside the palace.
📷 Gunawan Kartapranata · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#04VolcanoKaliurang

Mount Merapi Lava Tour

A 4x4 jeep ride on the slopes of one of Indonesia's most active volcanoes, past villages and riverbeds shaped by the 2010 eruption.

  • ⏱ 2-4 hours
  • 🚗 about 1 hr north of the city
  • 🌅 Early morning (clear views)
Our tipTours stop when the volcano's alert level is raised - we check the official status before you go. Bring a jacket for the cool morning air.
📷 Sam Hidayat · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#05AdventureGunungkidul

Jomblang Cave

A vertical cave you enter by rope with trained staff, then walk to a chamber where a beam of sunlight - the 'light from heaven' - falls through the roof.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 1.5-2 hrs from the city
  • 🌅 Late morning (light beam)
Our tipPlaces are limited each day and must be booked ahead. The light beam is strongest around midday in fine weather; wear shoes that can get muddy.
📷 Arie Basuki · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#06CoastGunungkidul

Timang Beach & the gondola

A wild limestone coast where a wooden gondola, first built by lobster fishermen, is pulled by rope over crashing waves to a small rock island.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 2 hrs from the city
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipThe last part of the road is rough - jeep transfers are common. Waves are strong: follow the staff and never swim here.
📷 Yasuspade · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#07HighlandsBantul

Mangunan Pine Forest & viewpoints

Tall pine forests and hilltop viewpoints in the Dlingo highlands, where morning mist fills the valleys - a favourite for sunrise and photos.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1 hr south of the city
  • 🌅 Sunrise
Our tipCombine it with the nearby viewpoints in the same morning, then head down for breakfast in the city.
📷 Nefrit Lazurit · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#08MuseumKaliurang

Ullen Sentalu Museum

A private museum of Javanese royal culture set in cool hillside gardens, telling the stories of the Yogyakarta and Surakarta courts through batik, paintings and letters.

  • ⏱ 1.5 hours
  • 🚗 about 1 hr north of the city
  • 🌅 Combine with Merapi
Our tipVisits are by guided tour only and photos are not allowed inside - a calm stop after the Merapi jeep tour.
📷 Rahmaziz · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#09Water funGunungkidul

Pindul Cave tubing

Float on a rubber ring along an underground river through a cave with stalactites, often combined with river tubing on the Oya River nearby.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1.5 hrs from the city
  • 🌅 Dry season
Our tipBring a change of clothes. After heavy rain the water can be high and trips may be paused.
📷 Tommy Gustaviano Yeza · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#10Food & shoppingCity centre

Malioboro & Beringharjo Market

Yogyakarta's famous main street: batik shops, street musicians, becak and horse carts, the traditional Beringharjo market and gudeg, the city's sweet jackfruit dish.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Evening
Our tipAgree the price before a becak or andong ride, and bargain politely in the market.
📷 Gunawan Kartapranata · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Practical tips

Getting there

Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) is west of the city - allow about 1-1.5 hours by road, or take the airport train. Trains from Jakarta and Surabaya also arrive in the city centre.

Best time

The dry season (roughly April to October) is best for temples, sunrises and caves. In the rainy season, plan outdoor visits for the morning.

Respect & dress

Cover shoulders and knees at the Kraton, temples and mosques; a sarong is sometimes provided. Use your right hand to give and receive, and ask before photographing people.

Getting around

The sights are spread out - a car with driver is the easiest way to combine them. In town, use ride-hailing apps, becak or andong (agree the price first).
