The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01Arts & cultureUbud, Gianyar

Ubud: palace, market & Monkey Forest

Bali's cultural heart: a royal palace with evening dance shows, a busy art market, the Sacred Monkey Forest and the Campuhan Ridge walk through green hills.

  • ⏱ Full day or more
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning (cooler, fewer crowds)
Our tipMacaques in the Monkey Forest grab phones, glasses and food - keep bags closed and do not feed them. Check the palace dance schedule for your dates.
📷 Chainwit. · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#02Sea templeTabanan

Tanah Lot Temple

A small Hindu sea temple perched on a rock just off the shore, with waves crashing around it - Bali's best-known sunset view.

  • ⏱ 1.5-2.5 hours
  • 🚗 about 45-60 min from Seminyak
  • 🌅 Late afternoon to sunset
Our tipAt low tide you can walk to the base of the rock; at high tide it is cut off. Wear shoes with grip as the rocks are slippery. The inner temple is closed to visitors.
📷 Grayswoodsurrey · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03Cliff templePecatu, Badung

Uluwatu Temple & Kecak dance

A sea temple on a high limestone cliff above the Indian Ocean, with a chanting kecak dance staged at dusk as the sun sinks into the sea.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-45 min from the airport
  • 🌅 Sunset
Our tipMonkeys here snatch sunglasses and phones - hold them tightly. The kecak show often fills up, so arrive early. Stay behind the cliff railings; sarongs are lent at the entrance.
📷 Paskuu · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#04Rice terracesTegallalang, Gianyar

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Steep green terraces stepping down a river valley just north of Ubud, with palm trees, walking paths and cafes looking over the fields.

  • ⏱ 1-2 hours
  • 🚗 about 20 min north of Ubud
  • 🌅 Early morning
Our tipGo early before the tour buses and photo-swing queues. Steps are steep and slippery after rain, and some path owners ask for a small donation.
📷 Vyacheslav Argenberg · CC BY 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#05Lake templeBedugul, Tabanan

Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, Bedugul

A water temple dedicated to the goddess of lakes and rivers, whose tiered shrines seem to float on Lake Beratan in Bali's cool central highlands.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1.5-2 hrs from Ubud
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipIt is much cooler than the coast, so bring a light jacket. Mornings are calmer, and mist can hide the view later in the day.
📷 LukeTriton · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#06Island day tripNusa Penida, Klungkung

Nusa Penida island trip

A rugged island with dramatic cliffs, the famous Kelingking T-rex headland and clear blue water, reached by fast boat from the mainland.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 fast boat from Sanur about 35-45 min
  • 🌅 Dry season, early start
Our tipKelingking's steps are steep and poorly kept - the viewpoint is enough. Island roads are narrow and rough, so use a driver, and never swim in strong currents. Rough seas can cancel boats.
📷 Georgi Kovachev · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#07Beach & sunsetKuta - Seminyak, Badung

Seminyak & Kuta beaches

A long sandy shore from Kuta to Seminyak with surf lessons, beach clubs, restaurants and shops, and sunsets that draw crowds onto the sand.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 15-45 min from the airport
  • 🌅 Late afternoon
Our tipStrong waves and rip currents are common - swim only between the flags near lifeguards. Traffic here is heavy, so allow extra time for transfers.
📷 Simon_sees from Australia · CC BY 2.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#08Calm beachSanur, Denpasar

Sanur Beach

A relaxed east-coast beach behind a reef, with a paved seafront path for walking and cycling, fishing boats, cafes and the harbour for Nusa Penida boats.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 20-40 min from the airport
  • 🌅 Sunrise / morning
Our tipThe water pulls far out at low tide, so swim around high tide. It faces east, which makes it a calm place for sunrise.
📷 Matt Croxson · Public domain · Wikimedia Commons
#09VolcanoKintamani, Bangli

Mount Batur sunrise trek

A pre-dawn climb of about two hours up an active volcano to watch the sun rise over Lake Batur and Mount Agung, often with a breakfast cooked by steam vents.

  • ⏱ Night to mid-morning (6-8 hrs)
  • 🚗 about 1.5 hrs from Ubud
  • 🌅 Sunrise (start about 3-4 am)
Our tipTake a local guide, a torch and warm clothes - the path is dark and the summit is cold. Batur is active, so we check the official volcano alert level before you go.
📷 WikiSantaClaus · CC BY 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#10UNESCOJatiluwih, Tabanan

Jatiluwih Rice Terraces

Wide terraced rice fields on the slopes of Mount Batukaru, part of Bali's UNESCO-listed subak water-sharing landscape and far quieter than the better-known terraces.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1.5 hrs from Ubud or Denpasar
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipStay on the marked paths - these are working fields, not a park. The fields change colour with the planting season, and we can tell you what to expect for your dates.
📷 chensiyuan · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Practical tips

Getting there

Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) is in the south, near Kuta. Seminyak and Sanur are usually 30-45 minutes away and Ubud about 1-1.5 hours, longer in heavy traffic. Many travellers also arrive by ferry from Java.

Best time

The dry season, roughly April to October, brings the clearest skies for beaches and volcano treks. The rainy season (about November to March) is greener, with showers mostly in the afternoon. Nyepi, the Day of Silence (usually March or early April), closes the airport and keeps everyone indoors for a day - we check the date for your trip.

Respect & dress

Wear a sarong and sash in temples (often lent at the gate), cover shoulders and knees, and do not step on the small offering baskets on the ground. Bali is mostly Hindu: halal restaurants and mosques exist in the main tourist areas, and menus may include pork, so ask if it matters to you.

Getting around

Sights are spread across the island and public transport is limited, so a car with driver is the easiest choice. Traffic is heavy around Seminyak, Canggu and Ubud. Scooters are popular but many tourists are hurt on them - we advise a driver instead.
