The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01Crater lakeCiwidey

Kawah Putih (White Crater)

A striking crater lake on Mount Patuha whose water shifts from milky white to turquoise-green, with steam and a faint smell of sulphur drifting over the shore.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1.5-2 hrs south of the city
  • 🌅 Morning (clearer, cooler)
Our tipStay on the paths, do not swim, and take a mask or scarf if you are sensitive to fumes. Access can be restricted if gas levels rise. Bring a jacket.
📷 Amelia guo · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#02VolcanoLembang

Tangkuban Perahu Volcano

A volcano named after a Sundanese legend of an upturned boat, with a road to the rim and a walk around huge craters, steam vents and a hot spring where visitors boil eggs.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1 hr north of the city (longer at weekends)
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipThis is an active volcano: the crater area has been closed at times when the alert level rose. We check the official status before you go. Expect persistent souvenir sellers.
📷 Vivi Lestari · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03Tea hillsCiwidey

Rancabali Tea Estate & Situ Patenggang

Rolling tea bushes in the cool hills south of Bandung, with Patenggang Lake at about 1,600 metres where you can take a small boat to the island of the legendary Love Rock.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 2 hrs south of the city
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipCombine it with Kawah Putih in one long day, starting early for clearer views. Paths can be muddy, and small cash notes help for fees and boats.
📷 Satria Budiana Tresna · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#04Family leisureLembang

Dusun Bambu & the Lembang highlands

A bamboo-themed leisure park in the Lembang hills with a lake, gardens, walking paths and restaurants - an easy, cool stop for families and a good base for the highlands.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs north of the city centre
  • 🌅 Weekday morning or lunch
Our tipThe Lembang road is crowded at weekends and on holidays, so go on a weekday if you can. Bring a light jacket for the evenings.
🏞️#05Old town walkCity centre

Braga Street & the Asia-Africa area

Bandung's colonial-era promenade with art deco facades, old bakeries and cafes, a short walk from Asia-Africa Street and the museum of the 1955 conference of Asian and African leaders.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Late afternoon to evening
Our tipWalk it in the late afternoon when it is cooler and the lights come on. Keep an eye on bags in crowded stretches, and mind the traffic when crossing.
#06LandmarkCity centre

Gedung Sate

Bandung's best-known landmark, an early 20th-century government building topped by a spire shaped like a satay skewer, with a museum about West Java inside.

  • ⏱ 1-1.5 hours
  • 🚗 about 10-20 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipIt is an office complex as well as a museum, so entry rules and open areas can change - check before you go. The lawn in front is a popular photo spot.
📷 Rahmatdenas · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#07Bamboo musicPadasuka

Saung Angklung Udjo

A cultural centre founded in 1966 where children and artists play the angklung, the bamboo instrument UNESCO recognised in 2010, in a lively show where the audience joins in.

  • ⏱ 2 hours
  • 🚗 about 20-40 min from the centre
  • 🌅 During a show
Our tipShow times have changed over the years, so we confirm the current schedule and book it for your dates. It suits families and is a gentle outing after a hiking day.
📷 dicky satarman from bandung, indonesia · CC BY-SA 2.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#08Shopping & foodCity centre

Cihampelas & factory outlets

Bandung's famous shopping streets: Cihampelas for jeans and clothes and Cihampelas Walk, plus clothing outlets along Jalan Riau, and many snack stops for local favourites such as batagor.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 15-30 min from the centre
  • 🌅 Afternoon
Our tipWeekends are jammed with day-trippers from Jakarta. Go on a weekday, compare quality before buying and bargain politely in street shops.
🏞️#09Forest walksDago

Dago Pakar & Juanda Forest Park

A forested park on Bandung's northern edge with waterfalls, walking trails and old wartime caves, near highland viewpoints and cafes with views over the city lights.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-45 min north of the centre
  • 🌅 Morning or sunrise
Our tipPaths are slippery after rain and cliff viewpoints can be dangerous - stay behind barriers. Take a torch if you visit the caves.

Practical tips

Getting there

The easiest way from Jakarta is the Whoosh high-speed train to Padalarang, then a short feeder train into Bandung, or a regular train or the toll road (about 2.5-3 hours by car). Flights have moved between Husein Sastranegara in the city and Kertajati farther out - check which one your flight uses.

Best time

The dry season (roughly May to September) gives the clearest views at the craters and tea hills. Bandung is cooler than the coast all year, and the rainy season (about November to April) brings frequent afternoon showers and mist, so do outdoor sights in the morning.

Respect & dress

West Java is mostly Muslim and Sundanese people are known for soft-spoken politeness: dress modestly, especially in mosques, and use your right hand to give and receive. Halal food is easy to find, and mosques and prayer rooms are common, including near the central square. Bring a jacket for the highlands.

Getting around

The main sights are spread north (Lembang) and south (Ciwidey) of the city, so a car with driver is the easiest option. Weekend traffic, especially on the Lembang road and from Jakarta, is heavy - weekdays are much calmer. In town, ride-hailing apps work well.
