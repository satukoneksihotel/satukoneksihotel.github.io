The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01LandmarkBukittinggi

Jam Gadang & Pasar Atas

Bukittinggi's famous clock tower, built in 1926 under Dutch rule, stands over a lively town square. Right beside it, Pasar Atas market sells Minangkabau textiles, snacks and crafts.

  • ⏱ 1-2 hours
  • 🚗 in Bukittinggi centre
  • 🌅 Late afternoon and evening
Our tipThe square is liveliest in the evening and at weekends. Markets are crowded, so keep bags closed, and ask your hotel which days the market is busiest.
📷 Rhmtdns · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#02Canyon & historyBukittinggi

Ngarai Sianok Canyon & Lubang Jepang

A deep green canyon some 15 km long runs beside the city, best seen from Taman Panorama. Close by, Lubang Jepang is a network of tunnels dug by forced labour during the Japanese occupation.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 on the edge of Bukittinggi
  • 🌅 Morning, for clear views
Our tipThe tunnels are narrow, damp and have many steps, so wear shoes with grip and think twice if you dislike enclosed spaces. Stay behind the railings at the canyon rim.
#03Crater lakeAgam

Lake Maninjau & Kelok 44

A deep lake that fills an ancient volcanic caldera, reached by the famous 44 hairpin bends of Kelok 44. Quiet villages, rice fields and viewpoints ring the water.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from Bukittinggi
  • 🌅 Morning, for clear lake views
Our tipThe descent is steep and winding, so drive slowly or use a driver. Puncak Lawang above the lake is the classic viewpoint, also known for paragliding.
📷 SpartacksCompatriot · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#04Royal heritageBatusangkar

Pagaruyung Royal Palace

A grand replica of the Minangkabau royal palace, with sweeping horn-shaped roofs and carved wooden walls. Inside, displays show traditional costume, ceremonies and the Minangkabau way of life.

  • ⏱ 1.5-2 hours
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from Bukittinggi
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipEarlier palaces were lost to fire, and the current building was rebuilt after a fire in 2007. Dress modestly and check opening times before you go.
📷 Rodney Ee · CC BY 2.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#05Cliffs & waterfallsLima Puluh Kota

Harau Valley

Sheer sandstone cliffs rise above rice fields in this nature reserve, with waterfalls dropping down the rock walls. A peaceful place for gentle walks, cycling and simple homestays.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from Bukittinggi
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipPaths can be slippery after rain, and the waterfalls run strongest in wetter weeks. Wear shoes with grip and keep to the marked trails.
#06Island hoppingPesisir Selatan

Mandeh Islands

A bay of green islands, mangroves and clear water south of Padang, often nicknamed West Sumatra's Raja Ampat. Hire a boat to hop between islands, snorkel and pass fishing villages.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 about 1.5-2 hrs from Padang
  • 🌅 Morning, when seas are calmer
Our tipBoat trips depend on the weather. Use a local operator that provides life jackets, check sea conditions that morning and skip the trip if the water is rough.
📷 Fandi Yogari Saputra · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#07Scenic roadLima Puluh Kota

Kelok 9 Viaduct

A curving flyover opened in 2013 that winds through forested hills on the main road between West Sumatra and Riau. The roadside viewpoint is a favourite photo stop.

  • ⏱ 30-60 minutes
  • 🚗 about 1.5 hrs from Bukittinggi, near Harau
  • 🌅 Daytime, in clear weather
Our tipThis is a busy route for trucks, so stop only at the proper viewpoint and watch for traffic. It combines easily with a Harau Valley day.
📷 Dela28 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#08Old townPadang

Siti Nurbaya Bridge & Padang Old Town

A 156-metre bridge over the Batang Arau river links Padang's old harbour quarter with Gunung Padang hill and is named after a classic Minangkabau novel. Lights and riverside snacks come out in the evening.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 in Padang city
  • 🌅 Late afternoon to evening
Our tipGo near sunset, when it cools down and the lights come on. Keep valuables out of sight in busy spots, and avoid the hill after dark.
📷 Indonesiagood · CC BY 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#09Beach & legendPadang

Air Manis Beach

A beach south of Padang with gentle waves and views of the hills, tied to the Malin Kundang legend of the son turned to stone. Small boats cross to the islands just offshore.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-45 min from Padang centre
  • 🌅 Late afternoon
Our tipThe Malin Kundang stone is a sculpture on the sand and is sometimes partly covered. Swim only where waves are gentle, and ask locals about currents first.
#10FoodPadang & Bukittinggi

Padang food trail & rendang

Minangkabau cooking is a reason in itself to come: slow-cooked beef rendang, rich gulai curries and the nasi Padang feast, where many small dishes are set out on your table at once.

  • ⏱ Any meal
  • 🚗 in both cities
  • 🌅 Lunch and dinner
Our tipIn hidang-style restaurants you pay for the dishes you touch, so send back what you do not want. Food is mostly halal and often spicy - ask for less chilli if needed.
📷 Midori · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Practical tips

Getting there

Minangkabau International Airport (PDG) lies north of Padang, about 40-60 minutes from the city by road, and an airport train also runs - check the schedule. Bukittinggi is roughly 2-2.5 hours from Padang by car; heavy rain can cause landslides on the highway, so allow extra time.

Best time

The drier months, roughly June to September, suit the highlands, Harau and the coast best. Rain can fall in any month, often in the afternoon in the highlands, and it is heavier towards the end of the year.

Respect & dress

West Sumatra is strongly Muslim and proud of its Minangkabau customs. Dress modestly away from the beach, cover shoulders and knees at mosques and palaces, and use your right hand to eat and to give. Halal food is everywhere and mosques are easy to find; pork and alcohol are hard to find.

Getting around

Sights are spread across the highlands and coast, so a car with driver is the easiest way to combine them. In towns use ride-hailing apps or taxis; in Bukittinggi, agree the price first for a bendi horse cart. The Mentawai Islands need a boat from Padang and extra days. Gunung Marapi near Bukittinggi is an active volcano, so check official status before any hike.
