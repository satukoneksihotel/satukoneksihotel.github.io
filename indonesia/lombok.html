The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01Car-free islandsNorth-west coast

Gili Trawangan, Air & Meno

Three small islands with white sand, clear water and sea turtles, where no motor vehicles are allowed - only bicycles, horse carts and walking. Trawangan is lively, Air relaxed, Meno the quietest.

  • ⏱ 1-3 days
  • 🚗 fast boat from Bangsal or Bali; about 2 hrs by road + boat from the airport
  • 🌅 Dry season; snorkelling in the morning
Our tipCurrents can be strong, especially on the east side of Trawangan and between islands - swim near the shore and use a guide for snorkelling. Take the official boat tickets, not offers from strangers.
📷 Vyacheslav Argenberg · CC BY 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#02South coastKuta, Central Lombok

Kuta Mandalika & Tanjung Aan

Kuta's pepper-like sand, Tanjung Aan's two-textured beach and clifftop hills such as Bukit Merese give wide, golden views. The area is also home to the Mandalika race circuit.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 30-40 min from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning and sunset
Our tipWaves and currents can be strong at some beaches - swim only where locals do, and avoid isolated spots at night. Beach sellers can be persistent; a polite no is fine.
#03Calm bayCentral Lombok

Selong Belanak Beach

A wide, curved bay of soft white sand and gentle waves, good for beginner surf lessons, families and slow swims. Fishing boats and green hills frame the view.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 45-60 min from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning or late afternoon
Our tipEven calm-looking water can have currents - follow flags and the instructors' advice. Shade is limited, so bring sun protection and water.
📷 Afifahdpl · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#04Volcano trekSenaru / Sembalun

Mount Rinjani & Senaru

Lombok's giant volcano holds a turquoise crater lake, Segara Anak, and a smaller volcanic cone. Even without a summit climb, the Senaru and Sembalun villages offer views, forest walks and cool air.

  • ⏱ 2-4 days for a trek; or a day in Senaru
  • 🚗 about 2.5-3.5 hrs from the airport to Senaru
  • 🌅 Dry season
Our tipRinjani is a serious trek. The national park can close routes for weather, fires or safety checks - we check current status for your dates. Book only a licensed operator and official permits.
📷 Midori · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#05WaterfallsSenaru

Tiu Kelep & Sendang Gile Waterfalls

Two waterfalls in the forest at the foot of Rinjani: Sendang Gile is the easier, closer one, and Tiu Kelep needs a longer walk and a stream crossing and has a cool swimming pool beneath it.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 2.5-3.5 hrs from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning, dry season
Our tipPaths are slippery and streams rise quickly after rain; take a local guide and avoid going in heavy rain. Wear sandals with grip and bring a dry bag.
📷 Jordy Meow · CC BY 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#06Traditional villageRembitan, Central Lombok

Sade Sasak Village

A living Sasak community with thatched-roof houses, woven cloth and customs passed down through families. A local guide explains daily life and traditional weaving.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 about 10-20 min from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipIt is a real home village, so dress modestly and ask before photographing people. Buying a handwoven piece directly from the weaver supports families, but there is no obligation.
📷 aainayyahm · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#07East coastJerowaru, East Lombok

Pink Beach (Tangsi)

A rare beach with a pink tint from tiny red coral fragments in the sand, beside clear water and dry hills. It is reached by a rough road or by boat from nearby harbours.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 about 2.5-3 hrs from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning, dry season
Our tipFew facilities and little shade - bring water, snacks and sun protection. Road and boat conditions change, so we check access and sea state for your dates.
🏞️#08Sunset coastWest Lombok

Senggigi & Malimbu Sunsets

Lombok's long-time resort strip: sandy bays along a winding coast road, beach cafes and sunsets over the Lombok Strait with Mount Agung on clear days.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 20 min from Mataram; about 1-1.5 hrs from the airport
  • 🌅 Sunset
Our tipThe coast road is winding, so take care at dusk, especially on motorbikes. Choose a beach cafe near your hotel and watch the sunset from the sand.
#09Grand mosqueMataram

Islamic Center Mataram

The Hubbul Wathan Islamic Center is Lombok's grandest mosque, with twin minarets and a golden dome. Lombok is nicknamed the Island of a Thousand Mosques, and this is its showpiece.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 in Mataram, about 1 hr from the airport
  • 🌅 Morning or evening
Our tipVisitors are generally welcome outside prayer times. Cover shoulders and knees, and women should bring a scarf. Check if the viewing tower is open on the day.
📷 Road97 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons

Practical tips

Getting there

Lombok International Airport (LOP) is in Praya, central Lombok - about 30 minutes to Kuta and 1 hour to Mataram. From Bali, fast boats and a ferry also reach Lombok and the Gili islands.

Best time

The dry season (roughly May to October) is best for beaches, boats and Rinjani. The wet season brings rain, rougher seas and trail closures, so plan flexible days.

Respect & dress

Most Sasak people are Muslim, halal food is everywhere and mosques are plentiful. Cover shoulders and knees away from the beach and in villages. Gili Trawangan is lively; Gili Meno and Gili Air are calmer.

Getting around

Lombok is large and sights are far apart - a car with driver is the easiest option. Ride-hailing is limited outside Mataram. On the Gilis there are no motor vehicles: walk, cycle or take a horse cart.
