The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01Botanical gardenCity centre

Bogor Botanical Gardens (Kebun Raya)

A garden of about 87 hectares founded in 1817, with giant trees, ponds, an orchid house, a memorial to Lady Raffles and shaded paths where you can spend a whole morning.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Early morning
Our tipGo early, before the day heats up and before afternoon rain, which is common in Bogor. Wear comfortable shoes, as the grounds are large; check the official site for opening days.
📷 Cep Budhi Darma · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#02PalaceCity centre

Bogor Palace & its deer

The presidential palace, built in 1856 as the Dutch governor-general's residence, stands beside the gardens. A herd of spotted deer roams its lawns and can be seen from the garden side.

  • ⏱ 1-1.5 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre, next to the gardens
  • 🌅 Late afternoon
Our tipIt is a working state palace, so the building is generally not open for ordinary visits - check the official site. The deer are most active in the late afternoon; do not feed them.
📷 Nafisathallah · CC BY 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03Mountain gardenCianjur

Cibodas Botanical Garden

A cool mountain garden at about 1,300-1,400 m, founded in 1852 as an outpost of the Bogor garden, with a moss garden, tree ferns and flowers at the foot of Mount Gede.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1.5-2 hrs from Bogor via Puncak
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipThe air is cool and mist can roll in, so bring a light jacket. Weekend traffic on the road up can double the journey - leave early or visit on a weekday.
📷 Sakurai Midori · CC BY 2.5 · Wikimedia Commons
#04Forest walkGede-Pangrango National Park

Cibeureum Waterfall & Mount Gede-Pangrango trail

A walk through mountain rainforest on the main Cibodas trail to the Cibeureum waterfall, passing the Blue Lake. Summit climbs of Gede and Pangrango continue from the same path.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 from the Cibodas gate, about 1.5-2 hrs from Bogor
  • 🌅 Early morning, dry season
Our tipThe park closes trails for the rainy season, bad weather, fires or maintenance, and summit climbs need a booking and permit - we check the official status. Stones are slippery, so wear grippy shoes.
📷 tigoretagore · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#05Safari parkCisarua

Taman Safari Indonesia

A large wildlife park of around 170 hectares on the mountain slope, with thousands of animals in open enclosures, animal shows and a drive-through area where you see them from a vehicle.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 about 1 hr from Bogor, on the Puncak road
  • 🌅 Weekday morning
Our tipArrive when it opens, because queues grow by midday and at weekends. Keep windows closed in the animal areas and follow park rules on feeding; we check current opening times.
📷 Arisdp · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#06Tea hillsPuncak, Cisarua

Gunung Mas Tea Plantation

Rolling green tea terraces beside the Puncak road, with guided walks through the fields, a footbridge across the plantation and a cafe where you can sit with a cup of tea.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from Bogor on the Puncak road
  • 🌅 Early morning
Our tipMorning gives the clearest views before cloud and traffic build up. Ask about the guided walk lengths and wear shoes that can get muddy.
📷 Baqotun0023 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
🏞️#07WaterfallsCisarua

Curug Cilember Waterfalls

A series of waterfalls among pine forest on the slope above Cisarua, reached by a stepped path that passes several cascades and shady rest spots.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1-1.5 hrs from Bogor
  • 🌅 Morning, weekdays
Our tipVery crowded on weekends and holidays. Steps and rocks are slippery, and water can rise fast after rain, so stay on the paths and out of fast water.
🏞️#08Theme parkSentul

Jungleland Adventure Park, Sentul

A big theme park in the Sentul hills with rides for all ages, themed zones, shows and a raft ride, a favourite with families and school groups.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 about 45 min from Bogor
  • 🌅 Weekday, cooler months
Our tipWeekdays are far quieter than weekends and holidays. Ride availability, height limits and opening hours change, so we check them for your date.
🏞️#09Food walkJalan Suryakencana

Bogor food: soto mie, asinan, laksa & lapis talas

Bogor's classics: soto mie, sweet-sour asinan salad, laksa with oncom and lapis talas, a taro layer cake bought as a souvenir. Jalan Suryakencana in the old Chinese quarter is the best food street.

  • ⏱ 2 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Evening
Our tipMany stalls on Suryakencana are Chinese-Indonesian and some serve pork, so ask before ordering if you need halal. Weekends are busy; a long queue is usually a good sign.

Practical tips

Getting there

Bogor is about 60 km south of Jakarta: roughly 1.5-2.5 hours by road from Soekarno-Hatta Airport (CGK) via the Jagorawi toll road, depending on traffic. Commuter Line trains from Jakarta run to Bogor Station. Puncak, Cisarua and Cibodas lie further up the old Jakarta-Bandung road.

Best time

Bogor is nicknamed the rain city: showers are frequent, especially in the afternoon, and the wet months run roughly October to April. The drier months, roughly May to September, are best. Go in the morning, and avoid Puncak on weekends and long holidays, when the road jams badly and police may run one-way or odd-even plate rules, so ask us for current rules.

Respect & dress

The region is mostly Muslim and Sundanese: dress modestly at mosques and villages and use your right hand to give and receive. Most local food is halal and prayer rooms are common, but some Chinese-Indonesian eateries around Suryakencana serve pork, so ask first. Puncak is cool, so bring a jacket.

Getting around

In Bogor city use ride-hailing apps or the local angkot minivans. Puncak sights are spread along one busy road, so a car with driver is the easiest way to link them and to time the traffic. Plan only two or three stops a day.
