The 10 places not to miss

Chosen for first-time and returning visitors. Add the ones you like to your trip - we turn them into a plan.

#01SunriseTengger Highlands

Penanjakan Sunrise Viewpoint

From the Penanjakan viewpoint, around 2,770 m high, the sun rises over the Tengger caldera: Bromo smokes in the mist, with Semeru on the horizon. One of Java's most famous views.

  • ⏱ Early morning (2-3 hours)
  • 🚗 about 3-4 hrs from Malang; 2.5-3 hrs from Probolinggo
  • 🌅 Sunrise (leave in the night)
Our tipIt is very cold before dawn - bring a warm jacket, gloves and a hat. Most visitors go by 4x4 jeep from Cemoro Lawang; arrive early because viewing spots fill up.
📷 Comepradz · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#02Active volcanoTengger Highlands

Mount Bromo Crater & Sea of Sand

Cross the grey Sea of Sand, pass the Hindu temple of the Tengger people and climb a stairway to the rim of Bromo's steaming crater.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 30-45 min from Cemoro Lawang
  • 🌅 Morning, after sunrise
Our tipBromo is active: the alert level can rise and access can close, so we check the official volcano status for your dates. Bring a mask or scarf for dust and gas, and stay behind the rim barrier.
📷 Thomas Hirsch (= user Ravn) · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#03GrasslandsTengger Highlands

Savanna & Teletubbies Hill

Rolling green hills at the foot of Bromo, nicknamed Teletubbies Hill, usually included in jeep tours. Lush in the wetter months and golden in the dry season.

  • ⏱ 1 hour
  • 🚗 on the jeep loop beyond Bromo
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipStay on the marked tracks and do not light fires or leave litter - dry grass burns easily. Mornings usually give the clearest light and the fewest clouds.
📷 Andhika bayu nugraha · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#04Hidden canyonProbolinggo

Madakaripura Waterfall

A tall waterfall inside a steep, round canyon: you walk along a stream and under curtains of falling water into a hidden amphitheatre of rock. Legend links it to the Majapahit minister Gajah Mada.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1.5 hrs from Probolinggo; about 3 hrs from Malang
  • 🌅 Morning, dry season
Our tipYou will get wet - wear sandals with grip and bring a rain jacket and a dry bag for phones. The path can close after heavy rain, so we check conditions for your dates.
📷 Christography82 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#05Thousand fallsLumajang

Tumpak Sewu Waterfall

A wide curtain of falls dropping into a green canyon beneath the Semeru volcano, often called the 'Niagara of Java'. A viewpoint above, and a steep trail down, give two ways to see it.

  • ⏱ Half day
  • 🚗 about 2.5-3 hrs from Malang
  • 🌅 Morning (rainy-season flow, dry-season access)
Our tipThe path down is steep, slippery and has ladders - go only with a local guide, in good weather, with sturdy shoes. Never go to the base after heavy rain because flash floods are possible.
📷 Muhyiddin · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#06Colourful villageMalang city

Jodipan Rainbow Village

Riverside homes painted in bright colours and linked by a glass-floored footbridge to the neighbouring 3D-mural village, Kampung Tridi. A lively, photo-friendly stop in the middle of Malang.

  • ⏱ 1-1.5 hours
  • 🚗 about 10-15 min from the city centre
  • 🌅 Morning or late afternoon
Our tipPeople live here, so keep voices low and ask before photographing residents. Check the official page for entry rules and the footbridge opening for your date.
📷 SoloMan22555 · CC0 · Wikimedia Commons
#07Highland fallsPujon

Coban Rondo Waterfall & Highlands

A forest-edge waterfall in the cool hills between Malang and Batu, with a short easy walk, a park with family activities, and nearby farms and coffee stops.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 about 1 hr from Malang city
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipRocks near the falls are slippery, so wear shoes with grip. Mornings are clearer; mist and rain are common by afternoon.
📷 Ivuvisual · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#08Family funBatu

Batu City: Jatim Park & Museum Angkut

Cool, green Batu is Java's family playground, with the Jatim Park attractions, the vintage-vehicle Museum Angkut, fruit farms and evening lights at the night market-style parks.

  • ⏱ Full day
  • 🚗 about 1 hr from Malang city
  • 🌅 Morning
Our tipWeekends and school holidays are very crowded - weekdays are calmer. Pick one or two attractions per day and check each official site for hours and any closures.
📷 Fathiya Rahmani · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Wikimedia Commons
#09Colonial heritageMalang city

Malang Old Town & Alun-Alun

Dutch-era boulevards, the Kayutangan Heritage street, the Tugu monument and tree-lined Ijen Boulevard make Malang an easy city to stroll, with bakeries, coffee and local bakso.

  • ⏱ 2-3 hours
  • 🚗 in the city centre
  • 🌅 Late afternoon / evening
Our tipEvenings are cool and good for walking. Traffic is busy at weekends, so use ride-hailing apps instead of driving into the centre.
📷 M_Sofyan_Sauri · CC0 · Wikimedia Commons

Practical tips

Getting there

Most visitors fly to Juanda Airport (Surabaya) and drive 2-3 hours to Malang; Malang also has its own small airport and a train station, and Bromo is reached from Malang, Probolinggo or Pasuruan.

Best time

The dry season (roughly April to October) usually gives the clearest Bromo sunrises, but it is cold - near freezing at times. The rainy season is greener but cloudier and waterfall trails are riskier.

Respect & dress

The Tengger people around Bromo are Hindu and the mountain is sacred to them; keep respectful near temples and ceremonies. Elsewhere the region is mostly Muslim, with halal food and mosques easy to find. Dress warmly and modestly.

Getting around

Bromo's sand sea is crossed by 4x4 jeeps from the village - your hotel or planner can arrange this. Between Malang, Batu and the waterfalls, a car with driver is easiest; in town use ride-hailing apps.
