Safety & alerts

Where to check government travel advice, weather, earthquakes, tsunamis and volcanoes before and during your trip.

Government travel advice (read at least one)

These are updated daily by foreign ministries and cover safety, local laws, health and entry rules for every country. They are written for their own citizens but useful for everyone.

Weather, earthquakes and tsunamis in Indonesia

Indonesia sits on the Pacific 'Ring of Fire'. BMKG is the official agency for weather, earthquakes and tsunami warnings; install its app or follow its site when travelling, especially in the rainy season (roughly November-March) and on coasts.

OfficialBMKG - Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics AgencyWeather forecasts, earthquake and tsunami alerts.bmkg.go.id ↗OfficialInaTEWS - Indonesia Tsunami Early WarningLive earthquake and tsunami warning system.inatews.bmkg.go.id ↗

Volcanoes

Several popular areas (Bali, Lombok, Flores, East Java, North Sumatra) have active volcanoes. Alert levels can close hiking trails and, rarely, airports. Check MAGMA Indonesia before a volcano hike or a flight during an eruption.

OfficialMAGMA Indonesia - volcano activityOfficial alert levels for every Indonesian volcano (Geological Agency).magma.esdm.go.id ↗

Disasters and crisis help

BNPB is the national disaster agency. In an emergency call 112 (see our Emergency page).

OfficialBNPB - National Disaster Management AgencyDisaster news and warnings.bnpb.go.id ↗
