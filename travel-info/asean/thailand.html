Entry

Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) is mandatory for all foreign visitors, by air, land or sea. It is free, online only, and can be submitted within 3 days (72 hours) before arrival (including the arrival day). Indonesians and most ASEAN citizens enter visa-free for short stays, and many other nationalities are visa-exempt too - the number of days has changed in recent years, so check the official e-Visa site for your passport.

Top cities and places to visit

Where most visitors go, and the places travellers find most attractive - based on tourism rankings and official tourism sites (checked October 2026).

1BangkokMost visited · Attractive

Thailand's capital and main gateway, known for grand temples, the Grand Palace, street food, night markets and big shopping malls.

2PhuketMost visited · Attractive

Thailand's largest island, popular for its beaches, island-hopping boat trips, resorts and lively nightlife.

3PattayaMost visited

A busy beach city close to Bangkok, known for family attractions, shows, water sports and day trips to nearby islands.

4Chiang MaiMost visited · Attractive

A cool northern city with an old walled centre, hundreds of temples, night bazaars and mountain trips.

5KrabiAttractive

Famous for limestone cliffs, clear water and easy boat trips to Railay, the Phi Phi Islands and Hong Island.

6Koh SamuiAttractive

A relaxed Gulf of Thailand island with palm-lined beaches, resorts and trips to Ang Thong Marine Park.

Culture - take note

The monarchy is deeply respected and protected by strict lese-majeste laws: never joke about or disrespect the royal family, images or banknotes (they carry the King's portrait). Dress modestly at temples (cover shoulders and knees; remove shoes). Do not touch anyone's head or point your feet at people or Buddha images. Monks must not be touched by women. The 'wai' (palms together) is the polite greeting. E-cigarettes and vapes are illegal. Alcohol sales have set hours and are banned on some Buddhist holidays.

Emergency

Tourist police 1155 (English). Emergency 191. Ambulance 1669.
