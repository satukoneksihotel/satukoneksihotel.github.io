Entry

Timor-Leste (East Timor) is Indonesia's neighbour and joined ASEAN in October 2025. Most guides say Indonesians can enter without a visa for a stay of up to 30 days, but the official immigration pages describe older arrival rules (an arrival card, proof of funds and a return ticket, and passport validity of at least 6 months, with a visa fee payable in US dollars cash for nationalities that need one). Confirm on the Immigration Service site whether Indonesians need a visa or pay a fee, before you travel. The US dollar is the currency: bring cash, because ATMs and money changers can be scarce, especially at border posts.

Top cities and places to visit

Where most visitors go, and the places travellers find most attractive - based on tourism rankings and official tourism sites (checked October 2026).

1DiliMost visited

The capital and arrival point, known for the Cristo Rei statue, waterfront, markets and the Resistance Museum.

2Atauro IslandAttractive

An island a boat ride from Dili, prized for coral reefs, diving, snorkelling, dolphins and simple eco-lodges.

3BaucauAttractive

The second city, with Portuguese colonial buildings, a natural spring swimming pool and nearby beaches.

4Mount Ramelau and MaubisseAttractive

Cool highlands with coffee villages and a sunrise hike up Mount Ramelau, the country's highest peak.

5Jaco IslandAttractive

An uninhabited island at the eastern tip, known for white sand and very clear water inside a national park.

Culture and practical - take note

A mostly Catholic country with strong community and church life: dress modestly in churches and villages, greet people politely and ask permission before photographing them or ceremonies. Tetum and Portuguese are official languages; Indonesian is widely understood by older people. Roads outside Dili can be rough and facilities limited - book transport and lodging ahead, and carry water and cash. Dengue and malaria precautions apply. If you cross the land border from Atambua (East Nusa Tenggara), check the border post's opening hours and the rules for vehicles first.

Emergency and help

Ask your hotel for the local emergency numbers and save the contact of the Indonesian Embassy in Dili before you go.
