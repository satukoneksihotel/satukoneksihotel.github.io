Entry

Guides disagree on how Indonesians get a Jordanian visa (on arrival for a fee, or arranged in advance), so confirm with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Jordanian embassy in Jakarta before you book. The Jordan Pass is the official bundle: it includes Petra and over 40 other sites and waives the tourist visa fee if you stay at least three nights - the validity rules changed in 2026, so check the Jordan Pass site for the current terms and prices. Security in the wider region can change quickly: check the Indonesian Foreign Ministry's advisory and other governments' advice just before you travel.

Top cities and places to visit

Where most visitors go, and the places travellers find most attractive - based on tourism rankings and official tourism sites (checked October 2026).

1PetraMost visited · Attractive

Ancient Nabataean city carved into rose-red cliffs, reached through the narrow Siq gorge to the Treasury.

2AmmanMost visited

Capital and main arrival point, with the Citadel, a Roman theatre, busy souks and good Middle Eastern food.

3Wadi RumMost visited · Attractive

Red-sand desert of towering rock formations, explored by jeep or camel with nights in Bedouin camps.

4Dead SeaMost visited · Attractive

Salt lake at the lowest point on land, where visitors float easily and try mineral-rich mud.

5JerashAttractive

Well-preserved Roman city north of Amman, with colonnaded streets, temples, theatres and Hadrian's Arch.

6AqabaAttractive

Red Sea port town for snorkelling and diving over coral reefs, and a handy base for Wadi Rum and Petra.

Culture - take note

Jordan is welcoming, Muslim-majority and conservative: dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered, loose clothes), especially outside the tourist resorts. The weekend is Friday-Saturday; Ramadan changes opening hours. Accept coffee and tea when offered - shake the cup gently to say 'enough'. Use the right hand and ask before photographing people. At the Dead Sea do not shave beforehand, keep water out of your eyes and do not stay in too long. At Petra walk early to avoid heat, wear sturdy shoes, agree prices before camel or horse rides, and note that Petra by Night is a separate ticket. Alcohol is available in hotels and licensed venues. Halal food everywhere.

Emergency

911 for emergencies (ask your hotel for the local police and ambulance numbers).
