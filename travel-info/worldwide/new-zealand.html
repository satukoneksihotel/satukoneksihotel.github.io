Entry

Indonesian citizens need a Visitor visa before travelling; the quick NZeTA is for visa-waiver nationalities and does not replace a visa for you. Apply online with Immigration New Zealand; check the current fee and processing time on its site (guides give different figures). Most visitors also pay the International Visitor Conservation and Heritage Levy - check the amount on the official site. Show funds, onward travel and a passport valid beyond your stay. Use only the official immigration.govt.nz site.

Top cities and places to visit

Where most visitors go, and the places travellers find most attractive - based on tourism rankings and official tourism sites (checked October 2026).

1AucklandMost visited

The largest city and main gateway, set between two harbours, with the Sky Tower, volcanic hills and island ferries.

2QueenstownMost visited · Attractive

A lakeside adventure town known for bungy jumping, jet boats, ski fields and day trips to Milford Sound.

3ChristchurchMost visited

The South Island's main city and gateway, with botanic gardens, punting on the Avon and access to the Southern Alps.

4RotoruaAttractive

Known for geysers, bubbling mud pools, hot springs, Maori cultural shows and traditional hangi feasts.

5Lake Tekapo and Aoraki/Mount CookAttractive

Turquoise lakes, starry skies in a dark-sky reserve and walks beneath the country's highest mountain.

6WellingtonAttractive

The compact capital, known for the Te Papa museum, a lively cafe scene, waterfront walks and film studio tours.

Culture and safety - take note

Biosecurity is extremely strict: declare ALL food, plant and animal items, and clean hiking boots and camping gear - fines are on the spot. Respect Māori culture: do not sit on tables, ask before entering a marae, and treat sacred (tapu) sites with care. Kiwis are relaxed; tipping is optional. Drive on the left; roads are narrow and winding - allow extra time. Outdoor safety matters: weather changes fast in the mountains, so register trip plans, carry layers, and use sunscreen (UV is strong). Sandflies bite at lakes and fiords - bring repellent. Earthquakes occur - know the 'drop, cover, hold' drill. Halal food is available in the big cities.
