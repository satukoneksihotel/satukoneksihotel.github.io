Entry

Most sources say Indonesian citizens need a visa for Zimbabwe, which can be applied for online (e-Visa, allow at least 10 working days) or obtained on arrival (reported fee about USD 30-50, stay about 1 month) - one site claims visa-free entry, which we could not confirm, so check with the Zimbabwe Department of Immigration or a Zimbabwean embassy before you book. The KAZA Univisa (Zimbabwe plus Zambia, for Victoria Falls) is for certain nationalities only - ask whether Indonesians qualify. Bring a passport valid for 6 months, a return ticket and hotel details. A yellow fever certificate is needed if you arrive from a risk country. Ask a doctor about malaria prevention.

Top cities and places to visit

Where most visitors go, and the places travellers find most attractive - based on tourism rankings and official tourism sites (checked October 2026).

1Victoria FallsMost visited · Attractive

Town beside the giant waterfall, with rainforest viewpoints, helicopter flights, Zambezi cruises, rafting and bungee jumping.

2Hwange National ParkMost visited · Attractive

Zimbabwe's largest park, known for big elephant herds, lions and wild dogs gathering at waterholes in the dry season.

3HarareMost visited

Capital and business hub, with the National Gallery, botanical gardens, stone-sculpture parks and busy markets.

4Lake KaribaMost visited · Attractive

Huge man-made lake on the Zambezi, best explored by houseboat for sunsets, fishing and wildlife on the shore.

5Great ZimbabweAttractive

UNESCO-listed stone ruins of a medieval African city that gave the country its name.

6Mana Pools National ParkAttractive

Remote UNESCO-listed floodplain on the Zambezi, known for walking and canoe safaris among elephants.

Culture and practical - take note

Zimbabweans are polite and well educated: greet first and shake hands. Avoid political discussion and do not photograph government, police or military sites; drones need permission. US dollar cash in small notes is widely used, and card machines and power can be unreliable - carry cash. Keep copies of your passport. Stay on guided walks and boats near wildlife, never swim in rivers (crocodiles and hippos) and do not walk at night. Victoria Falls is spectacular, but the spray in the wet season (about March to May) can hide the view. Tip guides and drivers. Halal food is limited outside Harare.

Emergency

Police 999. Ask your hotel or operator for ambulance and fire numbers.
