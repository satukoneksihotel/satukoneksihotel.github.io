Entry

Sources say Indonesian citizens need a visa for Armenia, either an e-Visa applied for online (reported fee about USD 8-38, up to 3 working days, stay reported from 21 to 120 days) or a visa on arrival at the airport and border points - the sources disagree, so confirm with the Armenian foreign ministry before you book. The official e-Visa portal is at evisa.mfa.am (we could not open it from our side, so type the address yourself and avoid copycat sites). Bring a passport valid for 6 months, a return ticket and hotel details.

Top cities and places to visit

Where most visitors go, and the places travellers find most attractive - based on tourism rankings and official tourism sites (checked October 2026).

1YerevanMost visited · Attractive

Pink-stone capital with the Cascade steps, Republic Square, museums, wine bars and views of Mount Ararat.

2Garni and GeghardMost visited · Attractive

Easy day trip to a first-century Greco-Roman temple and a UNESCO-listed monastery carved partly into rock.

3Lake SevanMost visited · Attractive

Large high-mountain lake with beaches, summer swimming and the hilltop Sevanavank monastery overlooking the water.

4EtchmiadzinAttractive

Spiritual centre of the Armenian Apostolic Church, with a UNESCO-listed cathedral counted among the oldest in the world.

5DilijanAttractive

Forested resort town inside a national park, with hiking trails, a craft street and the hidden Haghartsin monastery.

6TatevAttractive

Cliff-top medieval monastery in the south, reached by a long cable car over a deep gorge.

Culture and safety - take note

Armenia is one of the world's oldest Christian nations and proud of its history. In churches and monasteries cover shoulders and knees, men remove hats, and women may cover their hair - follow the signs. Hospitality is important: toasts at the table are a ritual, so sip slowly. Be respectful about the Armenian genocide when visiting the memorial in Yerevan. Avoid political topics about neighbouring countries. The land borders with Azerbaijan and Turkey are closed, and areas near them can be sensitive - check Kemlu and other advisories and keep to main roads. Do not photograph military sites. Mountain roads can be slow and icy in winter. Halal food is limited outside Yerevan.

Emergency

112. Police 102. Ambulance 103. Fire 101.
