Transparent booking procedure

Six steps from request to check-in

Who does each step, how long it takes and when your booking becomes final.

YouSatu KoneksiThe hotel
  1. 1

    Request

    You

    Duration: About 2 minutes

    • Tell us the city or hotel, your dates, guests and rooms - with the website form or on WhatsApp.
    • You get a request number and can follow every step in My Account.
    • Our team is told at once by WhatsApp and e-mail.
  2. 2

    Quotation

    Satu Koneksi

    Duration: As soon as we can during our hours

    • We check rooms and rates at several hotels and partners.
    • You receive one quotation: the total price for the whole stay, what is included and the pay-by date.
    • It appears in your account and on WhatsApp.
    Not reserved yet - no commitment
  3. 3

    Agree to book

    You

    Duration: Before the quotation's pay-by date

    • Read the quotation, what is included and the hotel's cancellation rules.
    • Press Agree to book in your account, or tell us on WhatsApp.
    • We send the bank details for this booking.
  4. 4

    Payment

    You → Satu Koneksi

    Duration: We check it during our hours

    • Transfer the amount to the bank account shown in your own request page.
    • Upload the transfer slip in your account.
    • We check the payment.
    Pay only to the account in your request page
  5. 5

    Booking confirmed

    Satu Koneksi + hotel

    Duration: After the payment is checked

    • We book your room with the hotel.
    • You receive a booking number - only now is the booking final.
    • Print or save the confirmation from your account.
    Booking final ✓
  6. 6

    Enjoy your stay

    You + the hotel

    Duration: Check-in to check-out

    • Show your booking confirmation and ID (KTP or passport) at reception.
    • Questions during your stay? Message us on WhatsApp.
    • Your request history stays in your account for the next trip.

Prices and payment

See price on a hotel shows the live online price for your dates, with nothing added by us. Your quotation is the total for the whole stay. We confirm the price again before you pay, because hotel rates change with dates and availability.

Payment is by bank transfer to the account shown in your request page. We never ask for your card number or your passwords.

Each hotel has its own cancellation rules. Before you pay, ask us for them - we write them in your quotation.
